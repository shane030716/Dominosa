How to play: Pair adjacent cells so every unordered pair 0–0 through 6–6 is used exactly once. Drag from one cell to an adjacent cell to confirm a domino. Clicking two adjacent cells makes a grey tentative domino, indicating it is not confirmed. Tap/click the space between two cells (or right along that cell edge) to connect them. Drag from one cell to an adjacent cell for a temporary connection. On computer, right-click between two cells to add/remove a gray bar. On touch screens, long-press the quarter of a cell facing a neighbor. Type two digits such as 00 to highlight all possible 0–0 domino locations. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo, Ctrl/Cmd+Y redo. Auto Assist: Quick Check automatically connects a domino when only one location remains; when you create either a confirmed or grey tentative domino, all other adjacent occurrences of the same number pair are automatically barred. Solve: works step-by-step using two basic rules plus two L/T/cross rules: a cell with one open side; a number pair with only one possible location; shared-centre elimination; and reserved-pair elimination. When those rules are stuck, Solve scans from the top-left for the first unsolved cell whose only open sides are right and bottom, guesses right first, and keeps solving. If that branch causes a contradiction, it returns to that guess and tries bottom. If it gets stuck again, it makes the next guess the same way.
